## Description (≤10,000 chars, plain text)
MTStreams is a movie and TV show discovery app for Windows. Browse trending films and series, search for any title, explore cast, seasons and episodes, watch official trailers, and open available playback sources from one clean, fast interface.

DISCOVER WHAT TO WATCH
• See what's trending now, what's coming soon, popular series, top-rated movies and animation picks on the home screen.
• Switch between movies and TV series in a tap, and keep browsing with suggestions for similar titles on every page.

SEARCH FILMS, SERIES AND PEOPLE
• Find movies, TV shows and actors quickly with a single search box.
• Filter results by movies, series or people to get to the right title faster.

EVERYTHING ABOUT A TITLE
• Storyline, release year, runtime, genres and audience ratings.
• Full cast and crew, with profiles that include biographies and the roles people are known for.
• Community reviews to help you decide what to watch next.

TV SERIES, SEASON BY SEASON
• Browse every season in the season library.
• Open the episode guide to see each episode's title, runtime and synopsis, then choose where to begin.

TRAILERS AND PLAYBACK
• Watch official trailers in a full-screen player.
• Open available playback sources and switch between them if one isn't working for you.

DESIGNED FOR WINDOWS
• A responsive dark interface that works well with a mouse, keyboard or touch screen.
• Previously loaded pages can still be previewed when you're offline.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION
MTStreams does not host, upload or own the movies and TV shows it lists. Playback is provided by independent third-party sources and is subject to their availability and terms, and it can change or stop without notice. Movie and TV information and images are provided by The Movie Database (TMDB). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
